📋 Product Master

Create each product once. Products are saved A–Z. During purchase, select the product and enter only Mfg. MM/YY and quantity.

➕ Add Purchase / New Batch

Select a product from Product Master. Unit and shelf life fill automatically.
Example: MFG 08/2026 + shelf life 12 months = expiry 08/27.
Enter MFG month/year and shelf life; expiry is calculated automatically as MFG + shelf-life months. If the same material + MFG month + shelf life + calculated expiry already exists, the purchased quantity is added to that stock entry.

⚙️ Data & Backup

Automatic backup is ON.
Every purchase, sale, stock change, new entry or deletion automatically updates a backup stored in this browser/device. For protection against device/browser loss, use Export Backup periodically.

☁️ Google Drive Auto Backup

After setup, stock changes can automatically update one backup file in your Google Drive. Google authorization is handled by the Apps Script you deploy.
